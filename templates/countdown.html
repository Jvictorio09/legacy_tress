Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about every piece of the system

What we build, run, and monitor each month to keep you at the top of every search surface that matters. Tap any question to see the detail.

What do 15 SEO/GEO-optimized curated articles per month do for my business?+

Regular, high-quality content signals to search engines that your site is active and relevant, helping you rank for more keywords and attract consistent, targeted organic traffic.

What is an advanced content cluster strategy?+

Structuring your content into tightly themed clusters builds topical authority, which improves your chances of ranking for competitive terms and keeps visitors engaged longer.

What's included in the Deep Competitor Search & Analysis Report?+

Detailed analysis of competitors' keywords, content, and backlinks reveals opportunities you can exploit and gaps you can fill to outperform them in search.

Why does extensive keyword research matter?+

Deep keyword research uncovers the full range of terms your ideal customers use, enabling smarter content targeting and more effective capture of high-intent traffic.

What is high-authority backlink outreach?+

Securing links from reputable, relevant sites boosts your domain authority and trust signals, directly supporting higher rankings and more referral traffic.

What is Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization?+

Optimizing your Google Business Profile makes you more visible in local map and pack results, increasing calls, website visits, and in-person enquiries from nearby customers.

Why 4 GBP posts per month?+

Consistent, informative posts keep your profile fresh and engaging, which can improve click-through rates and drive more actions like calls and direction requests.

What is GBP Q&A management?+

Proactively answering customer questions on your profile builds trust and removes confusion, leading to more qualified leads and fewer barriers to contact.

Why do I need 20 local citations?+

Accurate listings across key directories strengthen your local SEO footprint and NAP consistency, helping you appear more often in location-based searches.

What is technical SEO?+

Ongoing technical optimization improves your website's crawlability, indexability, speed, and overall performance. Fixing technical issues such as Core Web Vitals, schema markup, broken links, indexing errors, XML sitemaps, and mobile usability helps search engines rank your site more effectively while delivering a better user experience.

What does monthly AI visibility tracking and reporting include?+

Automated monitoring of rankings, traffic, and engagement gives you clear insight into what's working, so you can make data-driven decisions and continuously improve results.

What is priority support?+

Fast access to the support team ensures issues are resolved quickly and strategic adjustments can be made without delay, protecting your momentum and ROI.

Why 2 monthly strategy calls?+

Regular strategy calls keep everyone aligned on goals, performance, and next steps, ensuring the campaign stays focused and responsive to market changes.
